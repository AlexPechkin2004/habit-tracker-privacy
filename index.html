Habit Tracker Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 1, 2025

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Habit Tracker, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, use, and manage your data within our Android application.

Data Collection and Storage

All user data collected by the Habit Tracker application is stored in unencrypted form on Firebase, a secure cloud-based platform. The data we collect is solely used to personalize your experience within the application, such as tracking your habits and providing tailored insights.

Use of Your Data

We do not use your data for any purposes other than enhancing your experience with the Habit Tracker application. Your data is not used for our own purposes, nor is it shared, sold, or transferred to any third parties.

Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. If you wish to have your data deleted from our Firebase database, you can submit a request using the following Google Form: Data Deletion Request Form. We will process your request promptly and remove your data from our systems.
